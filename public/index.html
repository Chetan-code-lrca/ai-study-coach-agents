THE WORKFLOW

Everything has a place.
Every session has a purpose.

Study planning, learning from documents, practice, and progress belong together. Keep the loop simple enough to return to.

01

Plan realistically

Build a week-by-week study plan around your subject, current level, time, and goals—with room for review and catch-up.

02

Learn from your notes

Bring PDF, DOCX, text, Markdown, CSV, or JSON material. Ask for summaries, concept explanations, and answers grounded in the supplied text.

03

Practise with feedback

Generate multiple-choice quizzes, submit answers, inspect explanations, and revisit errors instead of only checking a final score.

04

Keep track of progress

Save your study profile, review recent plans, reopen saved plans, and examine quiz answers and scores over time.
